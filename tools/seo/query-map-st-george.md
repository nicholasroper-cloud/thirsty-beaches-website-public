### Evaluated and dropped (round two)
- Springdale and Zion: a gateway-town tourist market; vendors can't just set up in the national park, lodges and venues run their own food, and nobody searches for a soda trailer to book there. One line on the LaVerkin page and the hub ("ask and we'll quote the drive").
- Veyo, Dammeron Valley, Enterprise: small, far (Veyo is about 18 miles north on SR-18; Enterprise further), little to say that is verifiable and local. Named once on the hub as "further out, ask".
- LaVerkin and Toquerville as one page plus Leeds and Silver Reef as another: merged into one north-county page, because each alone is too small to carry 700 honest words.
- Little Valley as its own page: its substance is the tournament fields, which /st-george-tournaments-team-parties already covers; mentioned on the hub and the HOA page.
- Downtown St. George as its own page: the hub already covers Town Square and the Dixie Center; downtown is city-run events, not private bookings.
- Cedar City: outside Washington County. Skip until the trailer works there.

### Local facts and where they came from (opened 2026-11-16/17)
- Desert Color (lagoon about 2.5 acres, half-mile white-sand shoreline, "largest private body of water in the St. George area"; along I-15 and Southern Parkway, east of the interstate; Shores Resort pool-deck caf&eacute;; Atara restaurants; Crush Golf + Grill; pickleball park at Akoya Pearl Community Park with 6 courts, pavilion, lawn; ~6 miles of trails; events list; 55+ community; vacation rentals): desertcolor.com home, /about-us/, /desert-color-life/lagoon-pools/, /recreation/, /parks-trails/, /events/, /communities/atara/.
- Bloomington (Jamestown, Carpenter family, cotton, cane, molasses, brooms; renamed on survey; families gone by the 1950s; Terracor country-club community, "first planned community in the state of Utah"; Manzanita Road; Petroglyph Park): wchsutah.org/towns/bloomington.php.
- Green Valley (Snow ranch, 600 acres, annexed; Estates at Green Valley; The Park at Green Valley with owners association, clubhouse, pool, "many firsts for St. George"; Sports Village, Las Palmas, The Cottages, Green Valley Spa; condos rented to visitors): wchsutah.org/towns/green-valley.php.
- Santa Clara (Hamblin Indian Mission headquarters; Swiss converts; flood destroyed the fort; grapes; fruit and vegetable garden of the county for half a century): wchsutah.org/towns/santa-clara.php. Swiss Days (parade on Santa Clara Drive from the Jacob Hamblin Home to City Hall, Heritage Square, vendors via city sign-up, decorated cows): santaclarautah.gov/swiss-days. Lava Ridge Intermediate in Santa Clara: Wikipedia, Washington County School District (Utah).
- Ivins (Santa Clara Bench, Swiss-descended settlers, canal, named for Anthony W. Ivins, "Ivins, instead of Ivens"): wchsutah.org/towns/ivins.php. Tuacahn amphitheater, Kayenta Art Village and labyrinth, Fire Lake Park beach and swim area, resorts, Red Mountain Elementary: Wikipedia, Ivins, Utah. Kayenta 2,200 acres, 80% borders open space, HOA: kayentautah.com. Art village galleries, Center for the Arts, caf&eacute;: kayentaartvillage.com. Black Desert 19-hole course in lava fields: blackdesertresort.com. Ivins Heritage Days exists: ivinsutah.gov.
- LaVerkin (between Toquerville and Hurricane; farmland, surveyed townsite; canal, Fruit and Nursery Company, Pah Tempe hot springs, overlook, Hurricane-LaVerkin bridge): wchsutah.org/towns/laverkin.php. Toquerville (19 miles NE on SR-17; Chief Toquer; "toquer" means black; first cotton gin in the state; fruit; Kane County seat): wchsutah.org/towns/toquerville.php. Leeds (I-15 exits 22-23; Bennington; renamed for Leeds, England; grapes and wine; supplied Silver Reef; historic preservation ordinance): wchsutah.org/towns/leeds.php. Silver Reef Museum in the Wells Fargo Express building, county-owned monument: wchsutah.org/museums/silver-reef-museum.php.
- SunRiver (first 55+ community in southern Utah; 35,000+ sq ft community center with ballroom; over 70 clubs; poolside caf&eacute;): sunriverstgeorge.com and /why-sunriver/.
- St. George special event permits (public property incl. rights-of-way; park events beyond a normal reservation; apply 45+ days ahead; park reservations via Parks 435-627-4531): sgcity.org/specialevent.
- Tech Ridge on the former municipal airport site: techbuzznews.com "What's next for Tech Ridge". An airline headquartered in St. George: Wikipedia, SkyWest, Inc. St. George Regional Hospital (Intermountain): intermountainhealth.org (not named on the page). WCSD high schools: Wikipedia.
