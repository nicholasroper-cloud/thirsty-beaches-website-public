## What's a dirty soda, and why Naples needs one

A dirty soda is a fountain soda over nugget ice with flavored syrup, maybe fruit purée, and a pour of cream. It's Utah's signature drink, and most of Naples hasn't tried one. That makes it a crowd-pleaser at a party: nobody's seen it before, and everyone wants a second. The classic is our Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream). From there it's 114 drinks on the board, or [build your own](https://thirstybeaches.com/#build) and show us the screen.

**Where did dirty soda come from?**

Utah. Drive-thru soda shops there started mixing syrups, fruit purées and cream into fountain sodas around 2010, and it spread from there. It's still rare in Southwest Florida, which is half the fun.

**Is there something for guests who don't want soda?**

Plenty: soft-serve cones and cups, shakes, fresh espresso drinks, shaken lemonades and creamy Italian sodas, plus kid sizes.
