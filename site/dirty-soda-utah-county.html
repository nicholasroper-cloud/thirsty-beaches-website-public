Utah County is our home route. The trailer is based in Lehi and runs the whole valley, Provo and Orem to Spanish Fork and Payson, with dirty sodas built to order, real soft serve and fresh espresso.

The soda capital, with a trailer that comes to you

Provo and Orem probably have more soda shops per mile than anywhere in the country, so we're not here to explain dirty soda to you. What we add is the trailer. It parks at your reception, your ward's trunk-or-treat or your team's end-of-season party and pours for everyone, so nobody has to run a 40-drink order through a drive-thru. Order the classic Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream), pick from 114 drinks on the board, or build your own and show us the screen.

Floats, cones and real espresso

The trailer carries a real soft-serve machine, so any soda turns into a float, and there are cones, cups and shakes for the crowd that skips soda. We pull espresso to order for iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew too. For wards and school groups, the caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, zero-sugar sodas and Beach Babies kid sizes mean one trailer covers the whole room.

The parties Utah County actually throws

Wedding receptions and ring ceremonies. Ward, stake and youth activities. Missionary farewells and homecomings. PTA nights and booster fundraisers. BYU and UVU club socials, team dinners and end-of-semester parties. Office parties for the Orem and Provo tech crowd. We built the menu and the packages around exactly these, and we can name a custom drink after the couple, the team or the company. See event packages, or jump to weddings, school and church events or company parties.

Festival season, from Steel Days to Fiesta Days

Utah County summers run on city festivals: Steel Days in American Fork, Strawberry Days in Pleasant Grove, Summerfest in Orem, Art City Days in Springville, Fiesta Days in Spanish Fork, plus the Saturday farmers market in Provo. We're not open for public stops yet and we won't make up dates. When we book one of these, it goes on the homepage stops list and the live map. Drop your number there and we'll text you when the trailer is near you. Running a festival or market? Ask about a pop-up stop where guests buy their own drinks.

Questions

Utah County dirty soda FAQ

Is there a dirty soda trailer that serves Provo and Orem?

Yes. Thirsty Beaches is a retro dirty soda trailer based in Lehi that covers Provo, Orem and the rest of Utah County. We're opening soon for public stops and booking events now.

Can you cater a BYU or UVU event, club night or team party?

Yes. Student clubs, department socials, team dinners and end-of-semester parties all work. Most campus events need the organizer to clear food vendors and parking with the school, so start that early and we'll send whatever paperwork they ask for.

Do you do ward parties, trunk-or-treats and stake activities?

Yes. Ward and stake activities are a big part of what we do in Utah County. Caffeine-free drinks and kid sizes are on the menu, and you can cover the drinks or let families buy their own.

How far into Utah County will the trailer travel?

All of it. From Lehi and Saratoga Springs down through Provo, Springville, Spanish Fork and Payson. Lehi is home base, so north county is the shortest drive, and travel for the south end is included in your quote.

Can we book the trailer for a city festival or market?

Yes. For festivals, markets and fundraisers, we usually run a pop-up stop where guests buy their own drinks. Tell us the event and dates and we'll work out the vendor details with the organizer.

How much does it cost to book the trailer?

It depends on guest count, hours and what you add (soft serve, espresso). Send the date, place and head count and you'll get an exact price within one business day. No deposit is needed to ask.
