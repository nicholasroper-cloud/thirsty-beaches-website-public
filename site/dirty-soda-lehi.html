Lehi has soda shops. We're the one that drives to you.

Nobody in Lehi needs dirty soda explained. You can grab a Dr Pepper with coconut on almost any corner of State Street. What a drive-thru can't do is show up at your reception, your ward party or your office parking lot and pour for everyone at once. That's the whole idea of the trailer. Our Thirst Trap is the classic (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream), and from there the board runs to 114 drinks, or you can build your own and show us the screen at the window.

Floats and espresso, the part soda shops skip

We run a real soft-serve machine, so any soda on the menu can turn into a float, and there are cones, cups and shakes for anyone skipping the soda. We also pull espresso to order for iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew, which matters at a 9 a.m. office treat day. Zero-sugar sodas, caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, and Beach Babies kid sizes cover the whole family.

Built for Lehi events

For the tech crowd around Thanksgiving Point and Silicon Slopes, we do office treat days, launches and summer parties.
