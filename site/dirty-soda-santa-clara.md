## The town the Swiss settlers built

Santa Clara is older than most of the county. It began as the headquarters of Jacob Hamblin's Indian Mission on the Santa Clara River, and then a company of Swiss converts was sent south to settle it. Weeks after they arrived, a long rain flooded the river and destroyed the fort and the first settlement, and the earlier settlers moved over to the land where the Swiss families had settled. The Swiss knew how to grow grapes, and the warm climate turned out to be ideal for fruit; the Washington County Historical Society says Santa Clara became the fruit and vegetable garden of the county, and fruit stayed the town's main living for more than half a century.

## Swiss Days and Santa Clara Drive

The city's Swiss Days celebration honors those Swiss pioneers. The city describes a parade along Santa Clara Drive from the Jacob Hamblin Home to City Hall, pioneer activities at Heritage Square, live entertainment, local food and merchandise vendors, and decorated cows that residents and businesses display along Santa Clara Drive and in their yards. Vendors go through the city's own sign-up. If the trailer is ever part of a Swiss Days weekend, it will be because the city accepted us, and it will go on our stops list then. A private party at your own home is always an easy booking.

Summer afternoons here regularly pass 100 degrees, so we plan around the heat: a morning open house with iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew, or an evening party once the sun drops. Every drink is poured over nugget ice, and the soft-serve machine turns any soda into a float. For a midday event we'll suggest a short menu and a time window that keeps the line moving.
