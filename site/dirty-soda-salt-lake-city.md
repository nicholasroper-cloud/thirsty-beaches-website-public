## Salt Lake knows soda. We bring the shop to the party.

Salt Lake already has drive-thru soda on most corners, so we skip the explaining. What we add is a trailer that parks at your event and pours for everyone at once: a reception in Holladay, a block party in Millcreek, an all-hands in a downtown parking lot. The classic is our Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream). From there it's 114 drinks on the board, or [build your own](https://thirstybeaches.com/#build) and show us the screen at the window.

## Soft serve and espresso, in the same window

A real soft-serve machine rides along, so any soda can turn into a float, with cones, cups and shakes for the non-soda crowd. We pull espresso to order for iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew, which is why we work for a 9 a.m. office event as well as an evening reception. Zero-sugar sodas, caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, and Beach Babies kid sizes cover every age in the room.

## Salt Lake dirty soda FAQ

**Is there a dirty soda trailer in Salt Lake City?**

Yes. Thirsty Beaches is a retro dirty soda trailer that serves Salt Lake City and the rest of Salt Lake County. We're opening soon for public stops and booking events now.

**Can you do an office party downtown or in the Draper and South Jordan tech offices?**

Yes. [Office treat days, launches, client events and summer parties](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-company-party-utah). We park in your lot or loading area, pour for the team, and pack out clean. Fresh espresso makes it work for a morning event too.

**Do you cater weddings and receptions in Salt Lake?**

Yes. [Receptions, open houses, showers and ring ceremonies](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-wedding-catering-utah) anywhere in the valley. We can name a signature drink after the couple and keep a short menu so the line moves.

**Can you come to a neighborhood block party or HOA event?**

Yes. Block parties, HOA socials, school carnivals and ward activities all work. You can cover the drinks, open a tab up to a set amount, or let guests buy their own.

**How far around Salt Lake will the trailer travel?**

All of Salt Lake County, from downtown and Sugar House out to Millcreek, Holladay, Murray, Sandy, Draper, South Jordan and Herriman. Further out, like Park City or Davis County, [just ask and we'll quote the drive](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering?city=Salt%20Lake%20City#quote).

**Can the trailer set up downtown or at a venue with tight parking?**

Usually, yes. We need a flat spot we can drive a truck and trailer into, like a parking lot, loading area or venue pad. Downtown spots and some venues need a permit or a reserved space, so tell us the address when you [ask for a quote](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering?city=Salt%20Lake%20City#quote) and we'll sort out the setup and the price together.
