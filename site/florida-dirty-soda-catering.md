What shows up

## A Utah soda shop, parked on the Gulf Coast

Dirty soda is Utah’s drink: fountain soda over nugget ice, real syrups and fruit purées, and a pour of cream, every drink built to order. Thirsty Beaches brings it to Florida in a retro trailer, then adds the two things that matter in Gulf Coast heat. We run real soft serve, so any soda can turn into a float, and we pull fresh espresso for iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew.

Most Florida crowds haven’t had a dirty soda yet, so the trailer gets noticed. Pick a short list of easy wins like the Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream) or a pineapple-coconut Sprite, or run the full board. Zero-sugar versions, caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, and kid sizes cover everyone from the grandparents to the toddlers.

## Before you book

**What is a dirty soda?**

A fountain soda, like Dr Pepper, Coke or Sprite, mixed with flavored syrups or fruit purée and topped with a splash of cream. It started in Utah drive-thrus. See the full [menu](https://thirstybeaches.com/menu).

**How far ahead should I book?**

For weddings and busy winter-season weekends, a month or more out is ideal. For smaller events, ask anyway: if the trailer is free, we’ll come.

**Is this a soda truck I can rent for a party?**

Pretty much. It’s a retro soda trailer we tow to your event and run ourselves, so you get a full mobile soda bar with staff, not an empty truck. Birthday parties, HOA socials, weddings and company events all work, from Tampa Bay to Naples.

**How much does it cost?**

It depends on guest count, hours and what you add (soft serve, espresso). Send the form and you’ll get an exact price within one business day, with no deposit needed to ask.

**Can you set up at the beach?**

The trailer needs a flat, firm spot it can be driven to, like a parking lot, driveway, lawn edge or venue pad, so we usually set up at the venue or the closest lot to the sand. Tell us the spot and we’ll work out the best setup when we quote.

**Can guests pay for their own drinks?**

Yes. You can cover everyone, open a tab up to a set amount, or have us run a pop-up where guests buy their own. That last one works well for markets, HOA events and fundraisers.

**Do you have options for kids and people avoiding sugar or caffeine?**

Yes. Most sodas come in a zero-sugar version, lemonades and Italian sodas have no caffeine, and our Beach Babies sizes are made for kids.

**Do you do events in Utah too?**

Yes. The trailer opens first in [St. George](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-st-george) and also works Utah’s Wasatch Front: see [Utah catering](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering).
