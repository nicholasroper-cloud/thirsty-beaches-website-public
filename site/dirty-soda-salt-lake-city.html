Salt Lake knows soda. We bring the shop to the party.

Salt Lake already has drive-thru soda on most corners, so we skip the explaining. What we add is a trailer that parks at your event and pours for everyone at once: a reception in Holladay, a block party in Millcreek, an all-hands in a downtown parking lot. The classic is our Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream). From there it's 114 drinks on the board, or build your own and show us the screen at the window.

Soft serve and espresso, in the same window

A real soft-serve machine rides along, so any soda can turn into a float, with cones, cups and shakes for the non-soda crowd. We pull espresso to order for iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew, which is why we work for a 9 a.m. office event as well as an evening reception. Zero-sugar sodas, caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, and Beach Babies kid sizes cover every age in the room.

Where you'll find us around Salt Lake

We're not open for public stops yet, and we won't make up a date. Picture a Saturday float at the Downtown Farmers Market at Pioneer Park, a Thirst Trap after a lap around Liberty Park, an iced latte in Sugar House or on 9th and 9th, or a soft-serve stop down in Murray, Sandy or South Jordan. When we book a public stop, it goes on the homepage stops list and the live map. Drop your number there and we'll text you when the trailer is near you. Our home base is Lehi, so the south end of the valley is the shortest drive.

Questions

Salt Lake dirty soda FAQ

Is there a dirty soda trailer in Salt Lake City?

Yes. Thirsty Beaches is a retro dirty soda trailer that serves Salt Lake City and the rest of Salt Lake County. We're opening soon for public stops and booking events now.

Can you do an office party downtown or in the Draper and South Jordan tech offices?

Yes. Office treat days, launches, client events and summer parties. We park in your lot or loading area, pour for the team, and pack out clean. Fresh espresso makes it work for a morning event too.

Do you cater weddings and receptions in Salt Lake?

Yes. Receptions, open houses, showers and ring ceremonies anywhere in the valley. We can name a signature drink after the couple and keep a short menu so the line moves.

Can you come to a neighborhood block party or HOA event?

Yes. Block parties, HOA socials, school carnivals and ward activities all work. You can cover the drinks, open a tab up to a set amount, or let guests buy their own.

How far around Salt Lake will the trailer travel?

All of Salt Lake County, from downtown and Sugar House out to Millcreek, Holladay, Murray, Sandy, Draper, South Jordan and Herriman. Further out, like Park City or Davis County, just ask and we'll quote the drive.

Can the trailer set up downtown or at a venue with tight parking?

Usually, yes. We need a flat spot we can drive a truck and trailer into, like a parking lot, loading area or venue pad. Downtown spots and some venues need a permit or a reserved space, so tell us the address when you ask for a quote and we'll sort out the setup and the price together.
