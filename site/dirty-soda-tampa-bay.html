So what's a dirty soda?

It's a fountain soda over nugget ice with a flavored syrup or two, maybe a fruit purée, and a pour of cream. It started in Utah drive-thrus, and most of Tampa Bay hasn't had a proper one yet. Start with the Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream), the one everyone back in Utah orders. Or try a pineapple-coconut Sprite that tastes like it was made for Gulf heat. There are 114 drinks on the board, or build your own and show us the screen at the window.

Cold enough for a Tampa July

The trailer carries a real soft-serve machine, so any soda turns into a float. In August around the bay, that's a public service. Cones, cups and shakes cover the crowd that skips soda, and espresso pulled to order means iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew for a morning office event. Zero-sugar sodas and caffeine-free lemonades keep everyone in the room covered.

Tampa Bay dirty soda FAQ

Is there a dirty soda trailer in Tampa Bay?

Yes. Thirsty Beaches is a retro dirty soda trailer serving Tampa, St. Pete, Clearwater and the rest of the bay area, with soft serve and espresso on the same truck. We're booking events now.

Is dirty soda the same as an Italian soda?

Close cousins. An Italian soda is sparkling water with syrup and cream. A dirty soda uses a real fountain soda (Dr Pepper, Coke, Sprite, Mountain Dew) as the base instead. We pour both.

Can you cater a wedding or party in Tampa or St. Pete?

Yes. Receptions, showers, office parties, HOA socials, school and church events. Send the date, place and head count and we'll reply with a price within one business day.

Can you set up at Clearwater Beach or St. Pete Beach?

The trailer needs a flat, firm spot it can be driven to, so we set up in a lot, a driveway or a venue pad near the sand rather than on it. Beach parks and venues may need a vendor permit, so tell us the spot when you ask for a quote.

Can you serve a big crowd, like a school carnival or company picnic?

Yes. For big crowds we suggest a short menu of crowd favorites so the line moves fast, and we'll size the setup to your head count in the quote.

How far around the bay will you travel?

Tampa, St. Pete, Clearwater, Dunedin, Largo, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel and Apollo Beach are all in range. Further out, ask and we'll quote the drive.
