## From the Santa Clara Bench to Ivins

Ivins began as farmland on the Santa Clara Bench, settled about a century ago by families descended from Swiss immigrants, who farmed it with water carried by canal from the Santa Clara River. After the chapel was dedicated, the settlers chose to name the town after Anthony W. Ivins, and he agreed on one condition, according to the Washington County Historical Society: that they spell it Ivins, not Ivens. Today it is a growing city near Snow Canyon State Park, and the parties here still tend to happen at home, on a patio with a view of the red cliffs. That is where a trailer beats a drive-thru: we park, we pour for everybody, and nobody loads the kids in the car for a soda run.

## Kayenta and the art village

Kayenta covers about 2,200 acres of Ivins, and most of it borders open space. Its art village has galleries, a café, the Center for the Arts at Kayenta and a desert labyrinth laid out in small stones. It is one of the prettiest places in Utah to throw a reception, a shower or an open house, and a neon-pink trailer looks good in the photos against red rock. Kayenta has its own homeowners association, so check its rules on vendors and street parking before you book, and tell us where the trailer can pull in. More on [southern Utah receptions](https://thirstybeaches.com/st-george-wedding-soda-bar).

## Tuacahn, Black Desert and the resorts

Ivins is home to Tuacahn Center for the Arts and its outdoor amphitheater, and to resorts including Black Desert, with a golf course laid through the black lava fields, Red Mountain and others. Theaters, resorts and their restaurants run their own food and drinks, so we don't set up there without their invitation, and an event held there goes through the venue first. If you're staying in a rental or a family home nearby, a private party there is easy: check the rental's rules on vendors and parking, and we'll handle the rest.

## Fire Lake Park, Snow Canyon and the heat

Ivins has a beach of its own too: Fire Lake Park at Ivins Reservoir has a beach and a swim area. City parks have their own rules, so for a party at Fire Lake or another Ivins park we'll check what the city needs first. Snow Canyon State Park is on the doorstep, and commercial vendors need the park's permission to sell inside a Utah state park, so for a trailhead group or a race we'll meet you at a home or a private lot nearby instead. Summer afternoons pass 100 degrees, so we plan morning events with iced lattes or evening parties after the rock cools off, all over nugget ice.

## Ivins dirty soda FAQ

**Do you come to Ivins and Kayenta?**

Yes. Ivins is part of our Washington County home area, next to St. George, where we open first. [Send the date, place and head count](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering?city=Ivins#quote) and we'll reply with a price within one business day.

**Can you serve at Tuacahn or one of the resorts?**

Only if the venue invites outside food and beverage vendors. Theaters and resorts usually run their own food and drinks, so check your event contract or ask the venue first. Private homes and rentals are the easiest.

**Can you come to a home in Kayenta?**

Yes. Check the homeowners association's rules on vendors and street parking, and make sure a truck and trailer can pull onto a flat spot. Then send the head count and we'll quote it.

**Can you set up at Snow Canyon State Park?**

Not without the park's permission, since commercial vendors need it to sell inside a Utah state park. For a group day, we'll meet you at a home or a private lot nearby.

**Do you have drinks for kids and caffeine-free options?**

Yes. Beach Babies kid sizes, soft-serve cones, zero-sugar sodas, and lemonades and Italian sodas with no caffeine.
