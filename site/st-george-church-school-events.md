Church & school events

## Ward parties, youth nights and homecomings

Washington County’s calendar is full of church activities: ward parties and Christmas parties, Pioneer Day picnics, trunk-or-treats, youth nights, youth conferences, trek send-offs, and the missionary farewells and homecomings that bring the whole family to one backyard. A drink trailer turns any of them into an event people talk about, and in the town where Utah’s best-known chain started, a dirty soda is the treat everybody already wants.

If your activity is at a meetinghouse, the building’s rules come first, so ask your bishopric or building representative before you book. A covered tab with no sales is usually the simplest fit. If the building isn’t an option, a park pavilion, a school lot or a member’s driveway works just as well.

## An activity menu a bishopric can approve

We’ll put together a short, family-friendly menu that a bishopric, a Young Women or Young Men presidency, or an activities committee can sign off on quickly. It can be fully caffeine-free: lemonades, Italian sodas, caffeine-free soda bases and soft-serve cones. Zero-sugar versions and kid sizes come standard. Set a budget and we’ll cap the tab at it, so the ward clerk knows the number before the night starts.

## Schools across Washington County

PTA carnivals, back-to-school nights, teacher appreciation week, senior nights, and band, choir and drill banquets. PTAs, PTOs and booster clubs can book us at any school in the county, from Desert Hills, Snow Canyon, Pine View, Dixie and Crimson Cliffs to Hurricane, plus the elementary and middle schools that feed them. Schools and the district set their own rules for vendors on campus, so tell us what yours needs and we’ll work through it before we quote.

Few things say thank you to a southern Utah teacher like a dirty soda on a Friday afternoon. Pick the morning or the afternoon, we park at the school and pour for the staff, and the PTA covers the tab. Need an invoice or a purchase order? Ask in the notes.

## Farewells and homecomings

A missionary farewell or homecoming open house brings out the whole ward, the cousins and the high school friends, usually all at once. Park the trailer in the driveway and the line becomes part of the party. We can name a drink after the missionary or the mission, keep the menu short so the line moves while the family greets people at the door, and put everyone on one tab so nobody has to dig for a wallet.

## Utah Tech students and student wards

On campus, food and drink usually go through the university’s own dining and event services, so check with them first for anything held at Utah Tech. Off campus, student wards, clubs, intramural teams and apartment complexes book us directly for socials, back-to-school nights and finals-week study breaks.

## Fundraisers

Raising money for a class trip, a youth trek, a team or a cause? Ask about a fundraiser stop: we park at your event, guests buy their own drinks, and part of the sales goes back to your group. Fundraisers where guests pay work best at a school, a park or a business lot. Put “fundraiser” in the form and we’ll send the details. ## Before you book

**Can we leave caffeine off the menu?**

Yes. Tell us in the form and we’ll build a caffeine-free menu from lemonades, Italian sodas, caffeine-free soda bases and soft serve.

**Can you come to an activity at the church building?**

Ask your bishopric or building representative about the meetinghouse rules first. A covered tab with no sales is usually the simplest fit. If the building isn’t an option, a park pavilion or a member’s home works well.

**Can the ward, school or PTA pay by invoice?**

Ask in the notes and we’ll work out what your ward, school or organization needs when we send the quote.

**Do you do fundraisers?**

Yes. Guests buy their own drinks and part of the sales goes back to your group. Fundraisers work best at a school, a park or a business lot. Mention it in the form and we’ll share how it works.

**Can you serve at Utah Tech?**

On campus, check with the university’s dining and event services first, since they usually handle food and drink there. Off campus, student wards, clubs and apartment complexes can book us directly.

**Do you have kid sizes?**

Yes. The Beach Babies menu is sized and priced for kids, and soft-serve cones are always a hit at Primary and youth activities.
