## The beach town of Washington County

Thirsty Beaches was built around one idea, bringing the beach to you, and Hurricane is the town in Washington County with a beach of its own. Sand Hollow's red-sand shoreline and warm water pull boats, paddleboards and side-by-sides all summer, and Quail Creek is just around the corner. A cold dirty soda or a soft-serve float after a day on the water is exactly the drink we built the trailer for. Utah's best-known chain started here in Washington County, so the soda habit runs deep; what we add is real soft serve and espresso, poured at your party.

## Sand Hollow and Quail Creek: plan it the right way

Utah State Parks require permission (a special use permit) for commercial vendors, so we don't just pull onto the beach and start selling. For a big group day at Sand Hollow, talk to us early and we'll work through what the park needs with you, or we'll meet your group at your rental, a home or a private lot close by. The resort and golf venues near the reservoir run their own food and drinks, so private events there go through the venue first.

## Hurricane, LaVerkin and Toquerville

Hurricane has grown fast, but it still has its old downtown along State Street and a harvest celebration, Peach Days, that goes back generations. Washington County Legacy Park hosts the county fair, rodeos and markets, and county and festival events usually run their own concessions or vendor applications, so we check those first. Across the river bridge, LaVerkin, and up the road Toquerville and Leeds, are small towns with their own history and their own page: [LaVerkin, Toquerville and Leeds](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-laverkin-toquerville-leeds). In Hurricane we do backyard parties, ward activities, Hurricane High booster nights, HOA socials in the newer neighborhoods and [graduation open houses](https://thirstybeaches.com/st-george-graduation-birthday-party).

## Family reunions and vacation rentals

A lot of families gather in the rentals around Sand Hollow and Hurricane, with a pool, a big yard and three generations under one roof for the week. That's a perfect private booking: we pull up for a couple of hours, pour for everybody, and the cousins talk about the float bar for years. Check your rental's rules on vendors and parking, tell us the head count, and we'll quote it. Further out, like Springdale or the Zion side, ask and we'll put any travel in the quote.

## Trails, off-road weekends and the heat

Hurricane is a base camp for Gooseberry Mesa, the trails along the Hurricane Cliffs and the dunes out by Sand Mountain. Ride groups and off-road weekends start early and end hot. We open the morning with espresso and sweet-cream cold brew, then switch to dirty sodas, lemonades and floats over nugget ice as the day heats up. Summer afternoons here pass 100 degrees, so for a midday event we'll suggest a short menu and a window that keeps the line moving. Event organizers set their own vendor rules, so tell us the event and we'll check first.

## Hurricane and Sand Hollow dirty soda FAQ

**Do you come to Hurricane, LaVerkin and Toquerville?**

Yes. They're part of our Washington County home area, with St. George, where we open first. LaVerkin, Toquerville and Leeds have [their own page](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-laverkin-toquerville-leeds). [Send the date, place and head count](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering?city=Hurricane#quote) and we'll reply with a price within one business day.

**Can you set up at Sand Hollow State Park?**

Commercial vendors need the park's permission to sell inside a Utah state park, so a group day there takes planning. Ask early and we'll work through it with you, or meet your group at a rental, a home or a private lot nearby.

**Can you come to a family reunion at a vacation rental?**

Yes. Check the rental's rules on vendors and parking, and make sure a truck and trailer can pull onto a flat spot. Then send the head count and we'll quote it.

**Can you vend at a Hurricane festival or a Legacy Park event?**

Often, if the organizer approves us. County and festival events usually have their own concession or vendor rules, so we check what yours needs before we quote. We only list a public stop once it's booked.

**How do you handle the summer heat out here?**

Every drink is poured over nugget ice and soft serve is made on the trailer. For midday summer events we suggest a short menu and a morning or evening window so nobody waits long in the sun.

**When will the trailer be open?**

We're not open for public stops yet, and we won't make up a date. Stops go on the homepage stops list and live map the minute they're booked. Private events are booking now.
