Weddings

## The reception drink everyone will talk about

In Utah, the drink bar is often the main event at a reception, and a dirty soda trailer does that job well. Guests line up for a Thirst Trap or a Mountain Dew with mango and watermelon, the kids get soft-serve cones, and the photos look great with a neon-pink trailer in the background.

## Make it yours

We’ll name a signature drink after the two of you and match the syrups and toppings to your colors, so blush and gold or navy and silver both work. Keep the menu short so the line moves during the rush after the cake, or open the full board for a long open house. Add espresso if your crowd needs a pick-me-up for the late-night dance floor.

## Showers, luncheons and open houses too

Bridal showers, ring ceremonies, luncheons and backyard open houses can use the smaller Sip & Go package. Same trailer, shorter list and a smaller bill.

## Before you book

**Can you name a drink after us?**

Yes. Tell us what the two of you love, and we’ll build a signature drink and put your name on it for the night.

**How many guests can you serve?**

Every drink is built to order, so for big receptions we’ll recommend a short menu and the right number of hours to keep the line moving. Give us your head count in the form and we’ll plan around it.

**Can the drinks match our colors?**

Often, yes. Syrups, purées and toppings come in a lot of colors. Mention your palette in the notes.

**Do you do receptions outside Utah County and Salt Lake?**

Yes. St. George and Washington County are where we open first (see [southern Utah weddings](https://thirstybeaches.com/st-george-wedding-soda-bar)), and venues in Park City, Heber or Ogden are often doable too, and any travel is included up front in your quote.
