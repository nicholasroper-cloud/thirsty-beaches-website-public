# Privacy Policy

> What Thirsty Beaches collects, how we use it, and how to reach us.

Canonical page: https://thirstybeaches.com/privacy

The fine print


Effective October 25, 2026

Thirsty Beaches is a mobile dirty soda trailer. This page explains what we collect when you use thirstybeaches.com, order from our kiosk or your phone, or sign up for texts, and what we do with it. Short version: we collect as little as we can, we never sell it, and we never share your phone number with anyone for marketing.

## What we collect

- **Text and email alerts.** If you sign up, we keep your phone number or email address, the date you joined, and that you agreed to hear from us.
- **Orders.** When you order at the kiosk, on your phone, or ahead online, we get the drinks you picked and the first name you give us so we can call your order. Payments are handled by our payment processor, Clover. We never see or store your card number.
- **Punch card.** Your card lives in your browser under a random ID. If you choose to save it to your phone number, we store a scrambled (hashed) version of the number and its last 4 digits, not the number itself.
- **Event quotes.** If you ask for a catering quote, we keep what you type in the form (like your name, email, phone, event date and city) so we can reply.
- **Photos for drink matching.** "Match My Vibe", "Snap a pic" and the website's drink mixer send one photo to an AI service to suggest a drink. The photo is used for that one request and is not stored by us. Our AI provider may hold it for up to 30 days for safety checks, then deletes it. Ages 13+ only.
- **The basics every website gets.** Our hosting provider sees standard technical details like your IP address and browser type. We use them to keep the site running and to stop abuse, like too many requests.

## How we use it

- To make and hand you your drink, and to take payment through Clover.
- To text or email you about where the trailer is parked and our specials, only if you signed up.
- To answer your quote request and follow up about your event.
- To keep your punch card working, including moving it to a new phone.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We only share it with the services that run Thirsty Beaches for us, and only what they need: Clover (payments), Cloudflare (website hosting), our text message provider (sending texts), our email provider (sending email), and an AI provider for the drink-matching features. **We do not share your mobile number or text-message consent with anyone for their own marketing.**

## Text messages

Signing up for texts is optional and never required to buy anything. Message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply. Reply **STOP** to any message to stop, or **HELP** for help. The full details are in our [Text Message Terms](https://thirstybeaches.com/sms-terms).

## Keeping and deleting it

We keep alert signups until you unsubscribe, and quote requests and order details only as long as we need them to run the business. To see, change or delete what we have about you, email [hello@thirstybeaches.com](mailto:hello@thirstybeaches.com) and we'll take care of it.

## Kids

Our kiosk is family friendly, but our sign-up lists are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect contact details from children under 13. If you think a child signed up, email us and we'll remove it.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
