HOA & neighborhood parties

## A town of neighborhoods with a social calendar

A lot of St. George lives in neighborhoods with an HOA, a clubhouse and a calendar to fill: pool openings, movie nights, holiday parties, back-to-school bashes and welcome parties for the new move-ins. That is an old tradition here. When The Park at Green Valley opened, it came with an owners association, a clubhouse and a pool, which the Washington County Historical Society counts among the firsts for St. George. Today Desert Color runs summer kickoffs with food trucks and dive-in movie nights, and neighborhoods like Kayenta have homeowners associations of their own.

A drink trailer is the easiest crowd-pleaser on that calendar. We park at the clubhouse lot or the common area, pour every drink to order over nugget ice, and the whole street gets a drink built the way they like it. Real soft serve turns any soda into a float, and the kids get Beach Babies sizes and cones.

## 55+ communities and clubhouse socials

St. George’s active-adult communities run some of the busiest social calendars in town. SunRiver, the first 55+ community in southern Utah, has a community center of more than 35,000 square feet with a ballroom, over 70 clubs and groups, and its own poolside café, so the association sets the rules for vendors on its grounds, and a club or committee would go through it first. Desert Color has a 55+ neighborhood too. For an afternoon social, a club party or a street get-together with the association’s OK, we bring zero-sugar sodas, caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, and floats that remind everybody of the soda fountain they grew up with.

## How HOA billing works

The HOA can cover everyone, set a cap, or let residents buy their own. A pop-up where neighbors pay costs the HOA nothing. One quote, one invoice if you need it, and a clear price your board can approve. Tell us what your HOA or management company requires from vendors and we’ll confirm we can meet it before we quote.

## Clubhouse lot, common area or the street?

A clubhouse parking lot or a common area the HOA controls is the simplest spot: you approve it, and we need a flat place a truck and trailer can pull into, close to your guests. The trailer runs on its own generator and carries its own water, so no hookup is needed. A party that spills into a public street or uses a city park beyond a normal reservation can need a St. George special event permit, and the city asks for those applications at least 45 days ahead; park reservations go through City Parks. Washington City, Santa Clara, Ivins and Hurricane have their own rules.

## Apartments and townhome communities

Resident appreciation nights, pool-season kickoffs, move-in welcome events and leasing open houses work the same way, with the property manager covering the tab or residents buying their own. We follow your community’s rules on where to park and when to load in.

## Before you book

**Can the HOA cover everyone’s drinks?**

Yes. The HOA can cover everyone, set a cap, or let residents buy their own once the tab runs out. A pop-up where neighbors pay costs the HOA nothing.

**Do we need a permit for a block party?**

If the party stays on HOA common area or private property, your HOA’s rules apply. A party in a public street or a city park beyond a normal reservation can need a St. George special event permit, applied for at least 45 days ahead. Other cities have their own rules.

**Can you come to a 55+ community?**

Yes, with the association’s OK. Many active-adult communities have their own café or vendor rules, so a club or committee usually books through the association first.

**What do you need from the HOA?**

A flat spot a truck and trailer can pull into, like a clubhouse lot, and the HOA’s OK. Tell us what your HOA or management company requires from vendors and we’ll confirm we can meet it before we quote.

**Do you do apartment resident events?**

Yes. Resident appreciation nights, pool kickoffs and leasing open houses. The property manager covers the tab or residents buy their own.

**Do you have zero-sugar and caffeine-free drinks?**

Yes. Most sodas come in a zero-sugar version, lemonades and Italian sodas have no caffeine, and kid sizes and soft-serve cones cover the youngest neighbors.
