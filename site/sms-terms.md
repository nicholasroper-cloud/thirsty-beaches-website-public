**How often:** message frequency varies, usually a few times a month

**Cost:** message and data rates may apply

**Stop anytime:** reply **STOP** · **Help:** reply **HELP** or email [hello@thirstybeaches.com](mailto:hello@thirstybeaches.com)

## Signing up

You join by entering your phone number in the "Know before we roll up" form on [thirstybeaches.com](https://thirstybeaches.com/#stops) and checking the box that says you agree to get texts. Agreeing is never required to buy anything. We then send one text asking you to reply **YES**. You are not added to the list, and get no stops or specials texts, until you reply YES. You must be 13 or older and the number must be yours.

## Stopping texts

Reply **STOP** to any text and you'll get one last message confirming you're unsubscribed, then no more texts. You can also [unsubscribe on this site](https://thirstybeaches.com/unsubscribe) or email [hello@thirstybeaches.com](mailto:hello@thirstybeaches.com) and ask us to remove your number. To join again, sign up on the website.

## Getting help

Reply **HELP** to any text, or email [hello@thirstybeaches.com](mailto:hello@thirstybeaches.com).

## Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Texts are sent to US mobile numbers only.

## Your privacy

We never sell or share your mobile number or your consent with anyone for their own marketing. See our [Privacy Policy](https://thirstybeaches.com/privacy) for everything we collect and how we use it.

## Changes

If these terms change, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
