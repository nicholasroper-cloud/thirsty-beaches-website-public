What we make

## Soda, but dirty

A dirty soda is a fountain soda over nugget ice with flavored syrup, fruit purée and a pour of cream. We build every one to order from a mobile trailer, so the Dr Pepper is cold, the coconut cream is real cream and the lime is fresh. 114 drinks on the board, or build your own and show us at the window.

It doesn't stop at soda. We run real soft serve for cones, cups, shakes and floats. The espresso is pulled fresh, not pumped from a box, for iced lattes, cold brew and the sweeter stuff. Lemonades come shaken, Italian sodas come creamy, and the freeze-dried candy is made in house. Kids get their own sizes.

We open first in [St. George](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-st-george) and southern Utah ([Washington City](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-washington-city), [Santa Clara](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-santa-clara), [Ivins](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-ivins), [Hurricane](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-hurricane-sand-hollow)), and we book events on Utah's Wasatch Front (Lehi, Utah County and the Salt Lake area) and Florida's Gulf Coast (Tampa Bay, St. Pete, Sarasota and Naples). Farmers markets, ball games, church and school events, weddings, company parties. If there's somewhere for a trailer to park, we'll bring the beach to it.

## St. George dirty soda FAQ

**What is a dirty soda?**

A fountain soda over nugget ice with flavored syrup, fruit purée and a splash of cream. It started in Utah, and Utah's best-known chain started here in St. George. Dr Pepper with coconut and sweet cream is the classic, and our Thirst Trap is exactly that.

**Where is the trailer?**

We're opening soon. St. George is where we open first, and until then we're booking private events in St. George, the rest of Utah and Florida ([get a quote](https://thirstybeaches.com/#events)). Once we're open, the live map on this page shows exactly where we're parked and the stops list shows the rest of the week. Text and email alerts are coming soon.

**Can I book Thirsty Beaches for an event in St. George?**

Yes. Weddings, ward and school activities, team parties, HOA socials and company events across Washington County: [Washington City](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-washington-city), [Santa Clara](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-santa-clara), [Ivins](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-ivins), [Hurricane](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-hurricane-sand-hollow) and [St. George](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-st-george) itself, from [Desert Color](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-desert-color) to [Green Valley](https://thirstybeaches.com/dirty-soda-bloomington-green-valley). We also book Utah's Wasatch Front and Florida's Gulf Coast. [Send the date, place and head count](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering?city=St.%20George#quote) and we'll reply with a price within one business day.

**Do you have drinks for kids?**

Yes. The Beach Babies menu is sized and priced for kids, from a Lil' Root Beer Float to a Sandcastle Shake, and soft-serve cones are always a hit. Lemonades and Italian sodas have no caffeine, and most sodas come in a zero-sugar version.

**How much is a dirty soda?**

On our menu, dirty sodas are $3.49, kid sizes are $2.99 to $4.50 and soft-serve floats are $5.95 to $7.50. The whole board runs $2.95 to $9.99; see the [menu](https://thirstybeaches.com/menu) for every price. Events are quoted by guest count and hours.

**Can I build my own drink?**

Always. Use the builder on this page to pick a base, syrup, cream and topping, then show the screen at the window and we pour it exactly like that. You can share the build with a link.

**How do I pay?**

Tap to pay, cards and cash. Once we open you can order at the kiosk or the window, and the punch card on this site earns a free drink after ten.
