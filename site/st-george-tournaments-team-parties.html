Tournaments & team parties

St. George is a tournament town

When the Wasatch Front is cold, teams come south. St. George’s fields and courts fill up through the cooler months with softball and baseball, soccer and lacrosse, pickleball and youth mountain biking, and families drive down from Salt Lake, Las Vegas and Arizona to spend the weekend on the sidelines. The Fields at Little Valley, the Canyons Softball Complex and the trail park in Desert Color are the kind of places where a cold drink between games is worth a lot.

That’s the crowd we built the trailer for. Parents at a 7 a.m. first game want an iced latte. Players want a float after the last out. Little siblings want anything blue. We pour all of it to order from one retro trailer.

For tournament directors and complex managers

The simplest setup is a pop-up where families buy their own drinks, so it costs your event nothing. The trailer runs on its own generator and carries its own water, so we don’t need a hookup. Tell us what your venue requires, such as a vendor application, a certificate of insurance or a health permit, and we’ll confirm we can meet it before we quote. Some complexes have their own concession stand or an exclusive vendor; we’ll check that first, and we won’t set up next to your stand without your say-so.

We only post a public stop once it’s actually booked, so if you’d like the trailer at your tournament, get in touch early and we’ll work out the details with you and the venue.

Team parties after the last game

End-of-season parties, a post-tournament celebration at a park pavilion, a team dinner in a backyard, a coaches’ thank-you. The team or the club covers a tab (all of it, or up to a set amount), we park, and every player gets a drink their way. Floats and soft-serve cones tend to win. We have kid sizes for the younger teams and zero-sugar sodas for the parents keeping count.

Booster club and team fundraisers

Raising money for a travel season, new uniforms or a team trip? Ask about a fundraiser stop: we park at your event, families buy their own drinks, and part of the sales goes back to your team or booster club. Put “fundraiser” in the form and we’ll send the details.

What we need at the field

A flat spot a truck and trailer can pull into, close to where families walk between fields but out of the fire lanes and the parking aisles. We arrive well before the first game, set up the ordering kiosk and the window, and pack out after the last one, leaving the spot clean. If the venue wants us in a particular lot or behind a particular backstop, tell us when you send the details and we’ll plan around it.

Early games, hot afternoons

Tournament days in St. George can start cold and end hot. We open the morning with espresso and sweet-cream cold brew, then switch to dirty sodas, lemonades and floats as the day warms up, all over nugget ice. Caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas are on every menu. One honest note: a dirty soda is a treat, not a sports drink, so keep the water jugs on the bench. Questions

Before you book

Can you come to a tournament at Little Valley or the Canyons complex?

If the tournament director and the venue approve a food vendor for that event, yes. Tell us the tournament and the dates, and we’ll check the vendor rules before we quote.

Can parents and players pay for their own drinks?

Yes. A pop-up where families buy their own is the usual setup for tournaments. For team parties, the team or club can cover a tab instead.

Do you need power or water from the venue?

No. The trailer runs on its own generator and carries its own water. We just need a flat spot a truck and trailer can pull into.

Do you do team and booster club fundraisers?

Yes. Families buy their own drinks and part of the sales goes back to your team. Mention it in the form and we’ll share how it works.

Do you have caffeine-free drinks for players?

Yes. Lemonades, Italian sodas and several soda bases have no caffeine, and you can ask for a caffeine-free menu for a team party.

Can you do an early-morning game?

Yes. We pull espresso to order, so iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew are ready for the first game of the day.
