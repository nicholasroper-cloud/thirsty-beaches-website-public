What shows up

A soda shop that parks at your party

Thirsty Beaches is a retro drink trailer that brings the whole Utah dirty soda experience to your event: fountain soda over nugget ice, real syrups and fruit purées, a pour of cream, and every drink built to order. Then it does the two things a drive-thru can’t. We run real soft serve, so any soda can turn into a float, and we pull fresh espresso for lattes and sweet-cream cold brew.

You pick the menu. Run the full 114-drink board, or choose a short list of crowd favorites like the Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream) so the line keeps moving. Zero-sugar versions, caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, and kid sizes mean the grandparents, the toddlers and the Diet Coke loyalists all get something.

Questions

Before you book

How far ahead should I book?

For weddings and big Saturdays, the sooner the better. A month or more out is ideal. For smaller events, ask anyway: if the trailer is free, we’ll come.

Is this a soda truck I can rent for a party?

Pretty much. It’s a retro soda trailer we tow to your event and run ourselves, so you get a full mobile soda bar with staff, not an empty truck. Birthdays, graduations, reunions, weddings and company parties all work. Tell us the date and head count and we’ll quote it.

What’s the difference between a soda bar and a soda shop?

A soda shop is a drive-thru you go to. A soda bar comes to you: we park at your venue, pour drinks to order for every guest, and you don’t run anyone through a drive-thru line. Ours adds soft serve and espresso, which most soda shops don’t have.

How much does dirty soda catering cost?

It depends on guest count, hours and what you add (soft serve, espresso). Send the form and you’ll get an exact price within one business day, with no deposit needed to ask.

Can guests pay for their own drinks?

Yes. You can cover everyone, open a tab up to a set amount, or have us run a pop-up where guests buy their own. That last one works well for markets and fundraisers.

Can we choose the drinks?

Yes. Pick a short list from the menu, or let us suggest one for your crowd. We can also name a custom drink after the couple, the school or the company.

Do you have options for kids and people avoiding sugar or caffeine?

Yes. Most sodas come in a zero-sugar version, lemonades and Italian sodas have no caffeine, and our Beach Babies sizes are made for kids.

What do you need from the venue?

A flat spot for the trailer close to your guests, and a way to drive a truck and trailer in. We’ll confirm parking and power details with you when we quote.

Do you travel outside St. George, Utah County and Salt Lake?

Often, yes. Tell us where your event is and we’ll include any travel in the quote. St. George and Washington County are where we open first: see dirty soda in St. George. We also do events on Florida’s Gulf Coast, Tampa Bay to Naples: see Florida catering.
