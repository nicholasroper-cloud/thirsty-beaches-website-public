## So what's a dirty soda?

It's a fountain soda over nugget ice with a flavored syrup or two, maybe a fruit purée, and a pour of cream. It started in Utah drive-thrus, and most of Tampa Bay hasn't had a proper one yet. Start with the Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream), the one everyone back in Utah orders. Or try a pineapple-coconut Sprite that tastes like it was made for Gulf heat. There are 114 drinks on the board, or [build your own](https://thirstybeaches.com/#build) and show us the screen at the window.

**Is dirty soda the same as an Italian soda?**

Close cousins. An Italian soda is sparkling water with syrup and cream. A dirty soda uses a real fountain soda (Dr Pepper, Coke, Sprite, Mountain Dew) as the base instead. We pour both.
