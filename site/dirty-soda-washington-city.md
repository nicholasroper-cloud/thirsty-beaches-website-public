## Next door to where dirty soda started

Washington City sits just east of St. George along the Virgin River, and the soda habit here is as strong as anywhere in Washington County. Utah's best-known chain started here, a few miles down the road, and the drive-thru soda run is part of a lot of commutes. We're not a drive-thru. Thirsty Beaches is a retro trailer you book for your own event, and it pours for everyone at once. The Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream) is the classic, the board runs past a hundred drinks, and you can [build your own](https://thirstybeaches.com/#build) and show us the screen at the window.

## Neighborhood parties from Green Springs to Coral Canyon

A lot of Washington City is newer neighborhoods with an HOA calendar to fill: Coral Canyon out on the east end, Sienna Hills, Washington Fields, Green Springs, and the older streets around Telegraph and the historic downtown. Those are the parties we built the trailer for. A pool-deck social, a block party that deserves better than a cooler of cans, a back-to-school night for the street, a welcome party for the new move-ins. Tell us the head count and whether the HOA is covering the tab, we park on the street or in the clubhouse lot, and everyone gets a drink built the way they like it. Kid sizes and zero-sugar versions keep the whole street happy. More on [HOA and neighborhood parties](https://thirstybeaches.com/st-george-hoa-neighborhood-party).

## Made for Washington County summers

Summer afternoons here regularly pass 100 degrees, and an outdoor party needs cold drinks that don't take forever. Every drink is poured over nugget ice, the soft-serve machine turns any soda into a float, and cones and shakes cover anyone skipping the soda. For a midday summer event we'll suggest a short menu and a morning or evening window so nobody waits in the sun. Spring and fall evenings are close to perfect for a backyard party, and that's when a lot of receptions and open houses happen out here. Planning one? See [southern Utah weddings](https://thirstybeaches.com/st-george-wedding-soda-bar).

## Markets, Cotton Days and community events

Washington City has deep roots. The old cotton factory still stands near the historic downtown, and it's why the town's community celebration is called Cotton Days. The Saturday farmers market at Veterans Park, the community center and the city parks all host events of their own. Organizing a booth, a vendor row or a community night? Ask about a pop-up where guests buy their own drinks. Events and venues set their own vendor rules and permits, so we'll check what yours needs before we quote, and a public stop only goes on our list once it's booked.

## Washington City dirty soda FAQ

**Do you come to Washington City?**

Yes. Washington City, Coral Canyon, Green Springs, Sienna Hills and Washington Fields are next door to St. George, where we open first. [Send the date, place and head count](https://thirstybeaches.com/utah-dirty-soda-catering?city=Washington%20City#quote) and we'll reply with a price within one business day.

**Can you do an HOA or neighborhood party?**

Yes. The HOA can cover everyone, set a cap, or let neighbors buy their own. We need a flat spot that a truck and trailer can pull into, like a clubhouse lot or a quiet street.

**Do you do school and booster events in Washington City?**

Yes, when the school, PTA or booster club books it. Schools set their own rules for vendors on campus, so we'll work through what yours needs. Caffeine-free menus and kid sizes are easy.

**Can you vend at a market or community event?**

Often, if the organizer has room and approves us. Markets and city events set their own vendor rules and permits. We only list a public stop once it's booked.
