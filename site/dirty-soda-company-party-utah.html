Questions

Before you book

Can the company cover everyone’s drinks?

Yes. Cover the whole team, set a cap, or let us switch to guests paying once the tab runs out.

Can you send an invoice?

Mention it in the notes and we’ll set up billing your company can work with when we send the quote.

Do you do morning events?

Yes. Add espresso and cold brew with sweet-cream foam for morning all-hands, open houses and client breakfasts.

Can we name a drink after our company?

Yes. We’ll build a custom drink in your brand colors and name it for your company, product or team.
