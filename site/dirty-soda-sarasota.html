A Utah drink, made for Sarasota weather

A dirty soda is fountain soda over nugget ice with flavored syrup, maybe fruit purée, and a pour of cream. Utah made it famous, and it turns out it was built for a 92-degree Sarasota afternoon. Start with the Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream). Or go tropical with a pineapple-coconut Sprite or Mountain Dew with mango and watermelon. 114 drinks on the board, or build your own and show us the screen at the window.

Floats, cones and espresso

Drop vanilla or pineapple soft serve into any soda for a float, or skip the soda for a cone, cup or shake. The espresso is pulled fresh for iced lattes and sweet-cream cold brew, a good fit for a morning beach-house brunch or a long reception. Beach Babies kid sizes and caffeine-free Italian sodas keep the grandkids happy too.

Questions

Sarasota dirty soda FAQ

Is there a dirty soda trailer in Sarasota?

Yes. Thirsty Beaches is a retro dirty soda trailer serving Sarasota, Siesta Key, Lakewood Ranch, Bradenton and Venice, with soft serve and espresso on the same truck. We're booking events now.

Can you cater an HOA or community event in Lakewood Ranch or Venice?

Yes. Pool parties, clubhouse nights and neighborhood socials are a great fit. You can cover the drinks, run a tab, or have residents buy their own.

Can you do a beach wedding on Siesta Key or Lido Key?

We set up at the venue or the closest lot to the sand, since the trailer needs a flat, firm spot it can be driven to. Tell us the venue when you ask for a quote and we'll work out the setup.

How far ahead should I book in season?

For winter-season weekends (roughly November to April) and weddings, a month or more ahead is best. For smaller events, ask anyway.

Can guests pay for their own drinks?

Yes. You can cover everyone, open a tab up to a set amount, or have us run a pop-up where guests buy their own, which works well for markets and fundraisers.

What should I order first?

The Thirst Trap: Dr Pepper with coconut and sweet cream. It's the drink that made dirty soda famous in Utah. If you want something fruitier, try the pineapple-coconut Sprite.
