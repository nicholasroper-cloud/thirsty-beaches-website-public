What's a dirty soda, and why Naples needs one

A dirty soda is a fountain soda over nugget ice with flavored syrup, maybe fruit purée, and a pour of cream. It's Utah's signature drink, and most of Naples hasn't tried one. That makes it a crowd-pleaser at a party: nobody's seen it before, and everyone wants a second. The classic is our Thirst Trap (Dr Pepper, coconut, sweet cream). From there it's 114 drinks on the board, or build your own and show us the screen.

Soft serve and espresso on the same trailer

One window, three things: dirty sodas, real soft serve and fresh espresso. Turn any soda into a float, hand the kids a cone, and give the coffee crowd an iced latte or sweet-cream cold brew. Zero-sugar versions of most sodas make it easy for guests watching their sugar.

Naples dirty soda FAQ

Is there a dirty soda trailer in Naples, Florida?

Yes. Thirsty Beaches is a retro dirty soda trailer serving Naples, Bonita Springs, Estero and Marco Island, with soft serve and espresso on the same truck. We're booking events now.

Can you come to our HOA, clubhouse or country club event?

Yes. Community socials and pool parties are one of the best fits for the trailer. Many communities require vendors to be approved or insured, so tell us the community when you ask for a quote and we'll send what they need.

Where did dirty soda come from?

Utah. Drive-thru soda shops there started mixing syrups, fruit purées and cream into fountain sodas around 2010, and it spread from there. It's still rare in Southwest Florida, which is half the fun.

Is there something for guests who don't want soda?

Plenty: soft-serve cones and cups, shakes, fresh espresso drinks, shaken lemonades and creamy Italian sodas, plus kid sizes.

How far ahead should I book during season?

Season (roughly November to April) is when Naples calendars fill up. For holiday parties, weddings and big community events, reach out a month or more ahead. For smaller gatherings, ask anyway.

Do you travel to Fort Myers or Marco Island?

Yes. Fort Myers, Estero, Bonita Springs and Marco Island are all in range, and travel is included in your quote.
