Schools, churches & fundraisers

Family-friendly by design

PTA carnivals, back-to-school nights, teacher appreciation week, ward and stake activities, youth conferences, sports banquets and team fundraisers. These events need a treat that works for every age and every diet, and that’s exactly what the trailer does.

An event menu that’s easy to approve

We’ll put together a short, family-friendly event menu that a principal, a bishop or a PTA board can sign off on quickly. It includes caffeine-free lemonades and Italian sodas, zero-sugar soda versions, kid sizes and soft-serve cones. Caffeine on the menu is optional, and you decide.

Teacher and staff thank-yous

Few things say thank you to a Utah teacher like a dirty soda on a Friday afternoon. Pick the morning or afternoon, we park at the school and pour for the staff, and you cover the tab.

Fundraisers

Raising money for a team, a class trip or a cause? Ask about a fundraiser stop: we park at your event, guests buy their own drinks, and part of the sales goes back to your group. Put “fundraiser” in the form and we’ll send the details.

Questions

Before you book

Can we leave caffeine off the menu?

Yes. Tell us in the form and we’ll build a caffeine-free event menu from lemonades, Italian sodas, caffeine-free soda bases and soft serve.

Do you do fundraisers?

Yes. Guests buy their own drinks and part of the sales goes back to your group. Mention it in the form and we’ll share how it works.

Can the school or church pay by invoice?

Ask us in the notes and we’ll work out what your school or organization needs when we send the quote.

Do you have kid sizes?

Yes. The Beach Babies menu is sized and priced for kids, and soft-serve cones are always a hit.

Do you do church and school events in St. George?

Yes. St. George and Washington County are where we open first. See St. George church and school events and tournaments and team parties.
